about=Enables a map tool: clicking a plot queries the free web service of the
    Spanish Cadastral Electronic Office (Sede Electronica del Catastro) to
    get its cadastral reference, and opens its graphical record in the
    browser. A dockable side panel also lets you search a plot by cadastral
    reference or by rural poligono/parcela, marking it on the map first.
    Optionally shows whether the plot sits on a groundwater body, a
    flood-prone zone, or a Natura 2000 protected area, and whether it is
    classified as rural or urban land. Only covers the territory served by
    the Spanish Cadastre (not the Basque Country or Navarre, which have
    their own regional cadastre).
